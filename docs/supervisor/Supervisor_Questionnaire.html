Where the project stands today (for context)

Current digital twin

  • 0D (volume-averaged) global model of a low-pressure RF capacitively coupled argon discharge, following Lieberman & Lichtenberg, Ch. 10.
  • Inputs: RF power 50-300 W, pressure 1-20 mTorr (optionally an RF voltage for the Child-Langmuir sheath).
  • Particle balance (root-find) gives Te from pressure and geometry; power balance gives ne.
  • Gas temperature is fixed at 300 K; gas flow is recorded but has no effect.
  • Validated against Turner (2014) and others; 6 of 9 checks pass. The ionization rate coefficient Kiz is a "representative" fit, not from a cited cross-section set.
  • On top sits a machine-learning layer (classification, anomaly detection, uncertainty, recommendations) framed around semiconductor processing.

Proposed new structure

1
OES stage. Control RF power, pressure, gas (argon for now, other gases later) and flow rate (100 / 200 / 300 sccm). Outputs gas temperature, densities, etc.; graphs including the Boltzmann distribution / plot.
2
Underwater plasma using oxygen, for disinfection of waste.
3
Langmuir probe stage.
Our working assumptions (please correct): OES means optical emission spectroscopy; "Boltzmann distribution" means the Boltzmann plot of level populations; the existing 0D global model is the starting point for Stage 1 and will be extended rather than thrown away.
