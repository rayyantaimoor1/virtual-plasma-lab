Plasma Digital Twin - Measured Data Questionnaire

What real measurements exist, and can the project use them? · COMSATS University Islamabad

Why we are asking

The digital twin will be tested against real data wherever it exists. We need to know exactly what measurements are available (spectra, probe curves, disinfection results) and in what form. Questions marked IMPORTANT are the ones we most need answered.

How to complete this (3 steps)

  1. Answer every question. If you have nothing for a question, choose "No" or "Not yet" and write a short note.
  2. Click Save as PDF at the bottom. The page first checks that nothing is left blank.
  3. In the dialog set Destination to Save as PDF and click Save. Send that PDF back to the student.

The PDF contains every answer in full text. The "Review" section at the bottom shows exactly what it will contain.
